# Two Paydays

A household money planner for two people with two paydays. Enter each pay and it shows exactly what goes to the
shared accounts, the savings pots and the bills. Log spending as you go and it tracks every budget, pot, card and goal.

**Your data stays on your computer.** This repository holds only the app. Everything you enter is saved inside the
installed app on your own computer and is never sent anywhere.

## Install it on a Mac

Open the app's page (the GitHub Pages address of this repository, `https://<your-username>.github.io/two-paydays/`), then:

- **Chrome:** click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or open the ⋮ menu and choose
  *Cast, save and share → Install page as app*.
- **Safari** (macOS Sonoma or later): choose *File → Add to Dock*.

Two Paydays then opens in its own window from the Dock or Launchpad, and works without an internet connection.
Open it from that icon. (In Safari, the Dock app keeps its own data, separate from Safari tabs.)

## Your data

- **First time:** choose *Restore a backup* and pick your backup file, or start a blank plan.
- **Back up:** *Plan & settings → Backup, export and updates → Full backup*. The app reminds you once a month.
  Keep backups somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive.
- **Never put a backup file in this repository.** It is public.

## Updates

New versions are published to this repository. The app checks for them when it opens and every few hours, and shows
**Update now**. Before it updates, it keeps a copy of your data inside the app
(*Plan & settings → Backup, export and updates → Copies saved before updates*).
